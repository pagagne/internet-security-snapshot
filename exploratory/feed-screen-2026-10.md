# Feed screen, October 2026: how the candidates were sorted

First monthly run of the AI-attack feed screen (29 Oct 2026; 51 of 62 feeds answered; 65 candidates), sorted by Claude on 31 Oct 2026 by the method agreed with the author: duplicates merged; items already in the log dropped or used as second sources; news-site leads followed to the named organisation's own report; inclusion only where a real attacker used AI against real systems (campaigns, intrusions, stolen AI access) or an AI model in a test reached real systems (evaluation escape). Lab demonstrations, flaws in AI products, AI used as bait, defensive AI, marketing and opinion are excluded. New cases enter the log as "awaiting author check" with evidence unrated; the author confirms or excludes each in the 6 Nov check. Feed and article text was treated as data; none of it read as an instruction to an AI.

**Result:** 6 new log rows from the feed screen (Storm-0940, VAPOR PANDA, Wiz LLMjacking, Unit 42 CL-UNK-1211, Talos UAT-11985) plus 4 found while following its leads (CrowdStrike's four Claude Code harness actors); second sources added to 5 existing rows; 5 items for the author's judgement; the rest excluded with reasons below.
